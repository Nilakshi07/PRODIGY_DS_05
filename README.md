🚦 Analyzing Traffic Accidents: Road Conditions, Weather, and Time of Day 📊

Using a dataset of US traffic accidents from Kaggle, I explored how different factors like road conditions, weather, and time of day contribute to accident frequency and severity. This project allowed me to hone my skills in data analysis, visualization, and identifying patterns that could help improve road safety.



🔍 Key Steps Undertaken:

Data Loading and Cleaning: Loaded the dataset using Pandas and addressed missing values and incorrect data types to ensure the data was ready for analysis.

Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA): Applied Seaborn and Matplotlib to explore relationships between accident frequency and contributing factors such as road conditions, weather, and time of day.

Hotspot Visualization: Visualized accident hotspots by mapping accident locations across the US using geospatial visualization techniques.

Pattern Identification: Investigated how factors like wet/icy roads, fog, and nighttime driving correlate with accident frequency and severity, revealing important insights into high-risk conditions.



💡 Key Insights:

Weather Conditions: Accidents were more frequent during rainy or foggy conditions, with a noticeable spike in accident rates during icy roads in winter months.

Time of Day: Nighttime driving (especially between 6 PM and 12 AM) saw significantly higher accident rates, likely due to lower visibility and fatigue.

Road Conditions: Poor road conditions, such as wet or icy surfaces, were associated with higher accident severity and frequency, underscoring the need for better road maintenance and driver awareness.

Urban vs. Rural: Urban areas tended to have more accidents, but rural roads saw accidents with higher severity, possibly due to higher speed limits and less lighting.



📈 Learning Outcomes:

Data Visualization: Enhanced my ability to create effective visualizations using geospatial maps and other graphical techniques to uncover accident patterns.

Predictive Analysis: Gained insights into how certain conditions (like time of day or weather) can predict higher accident risks.

Safety Recommendations: Based on the findings, I suggested improving road safety in high-risk areas by raising awareness of weather and time-related risks and potentially increasing patrols during peak accident hours.
